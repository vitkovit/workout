3Nutrition

Your stated unknowns — calories, macros, intensity — answered with numbers. Track real intake vs weight trend for 2–3 weeks, then trust YOUR data over any formula.

Maintenance (TDEE)

BMR (nutritionist) ≈ 2,200 kcal · TDEE ≈ ~3,000 kcal (activity factor 1.36). Likely conservative for 5–6 lifts/wk + 8–10k steps. Real TDEE may be ~3,200–3,500 — track 7-day weight trend for 2–3 wk before trusting the number.

Fat-loss target (start)

Cut target ~2,400 kcal — 600 kcal deficit → ~0.5%/wk loss (~0.6 kg/wk). Textbook natural-comp pace (Helms <0.75%/wk to preserve muscle). Shrinks slightly as you lean: mid ~2,200, deep prep ~maintenance−300.

Protein (the anchor)

2.3–3.1 g/kg of lean mass (Helms). 80.8 kg LBM → ~200 g/day now, up to ~230 g in deep prep. Spread across 4–5 meals (~45–50 g each). Uric acid 4.33 mg/dL → no source restrictions. Collagen doesn't count — see supplements.

Carb cycling

Keep protein & weekly average fixed; trade carbs↔fat by day. Training days ~240–260 g carb / ~65–70 g fat. Rest days ~180 g carb / ~90–95 g fat. Put most carbs around training.

Rate of loss & diet breaks

0.5–1% bodyweight/week (~1 kg/wk early → ~0.4 kg/wk lean). MATADOR rule: every 6–10 weeks of dieting, take 1–2 weeks at maintenance — proven to retain more muscle, protect metabolism & adherence over a long cut.

⚙ How the app progresses loads — and why each phase needs a different Progression Profile

The app doesn't blindly add weight. After every set you rate it Easy / Good / Hard / Failed; next session the app adjusts the load by a % depending on the Progression Profile set on that program. The profile is a per-program setting in the app — the JSON import currently defaults to Standard, so you must change it manually under Program Detail → Progression Profile after importing each phase.

6Supplements — your stack, reviewed

You currently take vitamin C, collagen, creatine and beef protein, and feel that's enough. Mostly agreed — but two points genuinely affect the plan's anchor (protein) and are worth acting on.

Your stack — verdict

  • Creatine ✓ — already optimal at 3–5 g/day, every day (incl. through the cut). Nothing to change.
  • Beef protein — fine for hitting your target; just note it's typically lower in leucine than whey, so lean on whole-food protein (meat, eggs, dairy, fish) as the base and use the powder to top up.
  • Vitamin C — harmless; minor. Keep if you like it.
  • Collagen ≠ muscle protein. It's very low in leucine and an incomplete protein — it does not count toward your ~210 g target. Great for skin/joints, but don't let it pad the protein number.

Two worth adding (cheap, real evidence)

  • Vitamin D3 — 1,000–2,000 IU/day. You're a desk worker with low sun; deficiency is common and hurts recovery/hormones.
  • Omega-3 (EPA+DHA) — 2–3 g/day. Recovery, joints, cardiovascular — useful given the low cardio base you're building.

Don't bother / avoid

  • Fat burners · BCAAs (redundant) · "test boosters"
  • Any prescription diuretic for peak week — dangerous & banned.

✓ Eligibility: nothing in your stack is a banned substance — but still confirm against your target federation's list before entering.
